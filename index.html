What the platform helps you answer

Five questions that shape every screen in GeoNova AI.

1

Where might evidence justify further work?

Priority ranking built only from data you actually loaded, with the method shown in full.

2

What evidence supports an assessment?

Every result links back to the datasets, fields and records that produced it.

3

What is missing?

The Data Gap Detector separates “no evidence available” from “evidence did not support”.

4

What must be verified?

Field verification, sampling and expert review workflows with audit history.

5

How should resources be used?

Cost-aware planning that uses your own estimates — never invented market prices.

Evidence, clearly separated

GeoNova AI distinguishes the type and strength of every piece of information.

Observed

Imported or field-recorded evidence.

Derived

Calculated from observed values by a documented method.

Hypothesis

Unverified interpretation requiring field or laboratory confirmation.

Verified

Reviewed and accepted by an authorised professional.

Responsible AI statement. The local analysis engine is a transparent rule-based calculator, not a trained mineral prediction model. Connected AI services remain unavailable until an authorised backend endpoint is configured by your organisation.
Data privacy. This build stores everything in your own browser (IndexedDB, with a localStorage fallback). Nothing is transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly configure a backend endpoint in Integrations. Clearing browser storage will remove your data — export a backup first.
